# Workspace Guidelines: Essence Hair and Makeup Studio

1. **Design System & Aesthetics**:
   - Utilize guidelines from the `ui-ux-pro-max` skill for luxury beauty brand aesthetics (Playfair Display serif, Plus Jakarta Sans, Ivory, Warm Cream, Deep Espresso, and Brushed Champagne accents).
   - Maintain high contrast, mobile responsiveness, and zero layout shift.

2. **Motion & Interactions**:
   - Utilize **Framer Motion** for all component entries, hover states, and scroll animations (`whileInView`, `whileHover`, `whileTap`).
   - Utilize **VengeanceUI** and **Skiper UI** (`@skiper-ui/skiper40`) for animated border beams, interactive links, and polished micro-interactions.
